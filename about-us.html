Who are we?

We are two junior computer science scholars at Ignite Institute, Nahje and Joey. While we're not particularly passionate about coding, we both think that HTML and CSS are very neat, which is why we chose to make a website for our year long project. One of us in particular has a passion for Dungeons & Dragons, so the idea of a character website was pitched, and it stuck. Outside of D&D, we have a lot of shared interests, like anime and video games. As mentioned before, we also both like HTML and CSS, and recently we have been learning C++ which we enjoy as well.

What's our goal?

Our goal for this website is really simple. We did make this because we were required to for our year long project, but it goes deeper than that. we wanted to make something that was fun and entertaining. If we're going to make something, we don't want it to be something plain and dry just to get a grade and drop it. We wanted to make something that other people could find and enjoy. Plus, this website can help visualization and note taking for players in a future D&D campaign that one of us is holding, so we have more people to share it with other than just the judges and ourselves.
