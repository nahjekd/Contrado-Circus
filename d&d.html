What even is Dungeons and Dragons?

If you stumbled across this site, you may actually be wondering what Dungeons and Dragons is in the first place. While this isn't exactly the place to learn all of the knicks and knacks about the hobby, we can provide a general explanation as to what Dungeons and Dragons is.

Dungeons and Dragons is a tabletop roleplaying game, or TTRPG for short. Stories are led by a Dungeon Master, or DM, who will run a campaign. A campaign is made up of players, and the campaign or story focuses around these player's characters. One campaign is made up of multiple sessions, which usually last anywhere from 2-4 hours depending on the group. If a campaign only needs one session to be completed, then it is called a one-shot.

While there are officially licensed Dungeons & Dragons campaigns that can be run by a DM, many DMs opt to make their own story with its own content. This is called homebrew content, which just means that it was made by an individual player and is unlicensed. This is what Contrado Circus falls under. It is a homebrew story, written by one DM, in a carefully handcrafted world.
